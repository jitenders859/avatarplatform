Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

01 Overview

This policy explains what AvatarPlatform ("we," "us") collects when you create an account, build a chatbot, or interact with a chatbot embedded on someone else's website, and what we do with it. It covers the same service described in our Terms of Service — if the two ever conflict on a factual point, this page governs how your data is handled.

We are a small team; we don't have a dedicated legal department, and this policy is written in plain language rather than boilerplate. If anything here is unclear, email privacy@avatarplatform.ai and we'll clarify or fix it.

02 Who this applies to

  • Account holders — people who sign up to build and host a chatbot ("projects").
  • Visitors — anonymous people who talk to a chatbot embedded on an account holder's website. We process visitor data on the account holder's behalf; the account holder controls what their chatbot collects (see Section 5, Lead capture).

We do not store audio recordings from voice conversations — only text transcripts. We do not use your uploaded knowledge base content or conversation data to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.

04 How we use it

  • To operate the service: authenticate you, run your chatbots, answer visitor questions grounded in your knowledge base, and enforce plan limits.
  • To bill you, via Stripe, for paid plans.
  • To send transactional email: password resets, email verification, and — only if you opt in — usage-limit alerts.
  • To respond to support requests submitted through the contact form.
  • To keep the service secure: rate limiting, fraud/abuse detection, and audit logging of admin actions.

We do not sell personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

05 Lead capture — a note for visitors and account holders

Account holders can turn on "lead capture" for a chatbot, which prompts visitors to share contact details (typically name, email, phone) during a conversation. That data belongs to the account holder, not to us — we store it on their behalf so it appears in their dashboard, and it's covered by whatever privacy commitments the account holder makes on their own website. If you're a visitor and want data you shared with a specific chatbot deleted, contact the website you spoke with directly, or email us at privacy@avatarplatform.ai and we'll route the request to the account holder.

06 Who we share data with

We use a small number of subprocessors to run the service. None of them receive more than what's needed to provide their function, and none are permitted to use your data for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — hosts our Postgres database and file storage.
  • Google Gemini API — generates embeddings for knowledge-base search and powers AI chat/voice responses. Queries sent to Gemini are subject to Google's API terms; see Terms Section 6.
  • Stripe — processes payments and stores payment methods. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Optional, only if an account holder configures them for their own chatbot: ElevenLabs or OpenAI (alternate voice engines), Twilio (usage-limit SMS alerts), Serper (web-search grounding), Google Calendar (tour-booking tool).

We disclose data if legally required to (subpoena, court order) or to protect the rights, property, or safety of AvatarPlatform, our users, or the public.

07 Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The marketing and app pages use localStorage (not cookies) purely for functional preferences — your chosen theme (light/dark) and display language — which never leaves your browser and isn't read by us. The embedded chat widget uses a session identifier to keep a conversation's context; it's not used to track you across other sites.

If that changes — for example, if we ever add product analytics — we'll update this section and, where required by law, ask for consent first.

08 Data retention

  • Account data, projects, files, and conversation history are kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) permanently deletes all associated data — projects, files, embeddings, conversations, and leads — immediately. There is no 30-day recovery window, so export anything you need first.
  • Password-reset and email-verification tokens expire automatically (reset: 1 hour; verification: 24 hours) and are not reusable after use or expiry.

09 Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. For account holders, most of this is self-serve today:

  • Access / export: your dashboard shows everything tied to your account. A dedicated data-export button is on our roadmap; until then, email us and we'll send a copy.
  • Correction: update your name/email anytime in Settings → Account.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account → Delete account, or email privacy@avatarplatform.ai.

For anything not self-serve, email privacy@avatarplatform.ai — we'll respond within 30 days.

10 Children's privacy

The service is not directed at children, and account holders must be at least 18 (see Terms Section 3). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11 International transfers

Our infrastructure runs on US-based cloud providers (Supabase, Google Cloud, Stripe). If you're accessing the service from outside the US, your data will be transferred to and processed in the US.

12 Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever this policy changes, and for material changes we'll email account holders in advance.
